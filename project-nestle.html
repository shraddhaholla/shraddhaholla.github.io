FINANCIAL STATEMENT ANALYSIS · PROJECT 01

NESTLÉ VS. BRITANNIA

A Comparative Income Statement Analysis

FMCG · PROFITABILITY · COST STRUCTURE · 12M 2021–12M 2025

OVERVIEW

This project examines how two major companies in India's food and fast-moving consumer goods market performed over five comparable 12-month periods, from 12M 2021 to 12M 2025. It focuses on more than revenue growth: the analysis follows how sales moved alongside expenses, operating profit, profit before tax and net profit, and considers what those movements reveal about cost structure and profitability.

Using consolidated income statement data expressed in ₹ crore, the study applies comparative, horizontal, vertical and trend analysis. Together, these methods help compare the companies' financial scale, year-on-year movements, proportion of sales absorbed by expenses, and the direction of key profit measures over time.

Basis of comparison: Consolidated income statement figures for comparable 12-month periods, presented in ₹ crore. Nestlé India's financial-year transition included a 15-month period; a comparable 12-month series is used in this study, and the transition remains a limitation when interpreting year-on-year movements.

NESTLÉ INDIA

Nestlé India Limited operates in the food and beverage sector. Its product portfolio includes beverages, prepared dishes, dairy products, confectionery and nutrition-related products. The company has an established manufacturing and distribution network and a significant presence in the Indian food market.

BRITANNIA INDUSTRIES

Britannia Industries Limited is an established Indian food company with major product categories including biscuits, dairy products, breads, rusk, cakes and snacking products. Its performance is shaped by competition across consumer food categories, distribution reach, pricing and input-cost management.
